It started with a social app scroll.

One afternoon during summer break, I was scrolling through a social app when I came across a freelance opportunity. I applied without expecting much. My entire business plan at the time was “let’s see what happens.” Somehow, that was enough to get me started.

I still remember my first payment. It was ₹6,000 for building a portfolio website for a graphic designer. Seeing that payment come in made freelancing feel like something I could actually do. After that, I started taking on more projects and got to work with different businesses, learning a little more with each one.

I’m grateful to God for how far that first opportunity has taken me. If there’s one thing I’d tell someone who’s waiting to feel ready, it’s to give it a shot anyway. I was just scrolling that afternoon. I’m glad I stopped long enough to apply.
